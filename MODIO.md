# Own Tile Indicator - mod.io page

## Summary

Marks the tile your character is standing on.

## Description

Own Tile Indicator draws an outline on the tile under your character and moves with you as you walk, run or teleport. It makes it easy to see exactly which tile you're on, for example when lining up next to a boss or a skilling spot.

### Features

**Marker**

**Your tile**
The tile under your character is outlined. Walk two tiles east and the outline moves two tiles east with you.

**Follows the ground**
Each corner sits at the ground height of that corner, so on a slope the marker tilts with the ground instead of disappearing into it.

**Show through objects**
On by default, so the marker stays visible under your character, tables and walls. Turn it off and it sits behind things like normal ground.

**Fade when standing still**
After you stand still for 3 seconds the marker smoothly fades out, so it stays out of the way while you skill or bank. Take a step and it fades straight back in. The delay can be set from 1 second up to a minute, or turn off Fade when still so the marker always shows.

**Look**

**Colour**
Cyan, White, Green, Yellow, Orange, Red, Pink or Purple. Each click on the colour button moves to the next one, and the swatch next to it shows the current colour.

**Fill**
Off, Light, Medium or Strong. Light gives a faint tint on the tile, Strong makes it easy to spot in busy areas.

**Line width**
From 1 to 5 with the - and + buttons. The default is 2.

**Corners only**
Draws just the four corners instead of the full outline, for a cleaner look.

### Getting started

1. Enable Own Tile Indicator under Manage in the Plugin Hub.
2. Your tile is marked straight away.
3. To change the colour, fill, line width, corners or fade, click the Own Tile Indicator icon in the Plugins window, or the cog next to it under Manage.

### Good to know

* The marker follows your character model, so while you're moving it switches tile when your character visibly crosses the edge. It doesn't show where the server has you.
* While the settings page is open, any change brings the marker back for a moment so you can see how it looks.
* Reset to defaults at the bottom of the settings puts everything back to how it started.
* It only marks your own tile. To mark other tiles, use a tile marker plugin.
* The marker is hidden on the login screen and in the lobby, and while the area around you is loading.
* It doesn't need PrettyUI or any other plugin.

### Permissions

This plugin requests no permissions and makes no network requests.

### Data protection

Own Tile Indicator only reads your character's position to draw the marker on your own screen. Your colour, fill, line width, corners, show through objects and fade settings are saved locally in the client's plugin storage. Nothing leaves your computer and nothing is shared with the developer or anyone else. You can change any setting on the settings page, or remove everything by uninstalling the plugin.
