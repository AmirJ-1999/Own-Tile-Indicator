# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First version.

NEW
- Outline on the tile under your character that follows you around
- Follows the slope of the ground
- Colour, fill, line width and corners only settings
- Show through objects, on by default
- Fades out when standing still, with a delay from 1 second to a minute
- Settings in the Plugins window, with a reset to defaults button
